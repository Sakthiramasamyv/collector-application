Appointment Booking Guide - District Collector's Office
========================================================

ABOUT MEETING THE DISTRICT COLLECTOR:
The District Collector holds public hearings every Monday (10 AM - 1 PM).
For urgent matters, special appointments can be requested through this portal.

TYPES OF APPOINTMENTS:
1. General Public Grievance Meeting (Monday Public Hearing)
   - Walk-in or pre-register online
   - Duration: 10 minutes per person
   - No prior appointment needed for Monday hearing

2. Formal Appointment Request
   - For complex cases requiring detailed discussion
   - Requires prior application and approval
   - Documents must be submitted in advance
   - Response within 2-3 working days

3. Emergency Appointment
   - For life-threatening or critical situations only
   - Contact Control Room: 0422-2390111
   - Available 24x7 for genuine emergencies

APPOINTMENT REQUEST PROCESS:
Step 1: Fill appointment request form on this portal
Step 2: Describe the purpose clearly (minimum 50 words)
Step 3: Upload supporting documents if any
Step 4: Receive appointment ID (APT-XXXX)
Step 5: Collector's PA reviews within 24-48 hours
Step 6: Appointment date/time confirmed via SMS/email
Step 7: Visit collectorate with appointment confirmation

WHAT TO BRING FOR APPOINTMENT:
- Government-issued ID proof (Aadhaar/Voter ID)
- Appointment confirmation slip
- All relevant documents for your issue
- Previous complaint ticket numbers if any
- Two passport size photographs

APPOINTMENT RULES & ETIQUETTE:
- Arrive 15 minutes before scheduled time
- Dress code: Formal/Traditional attire
- Mobile phones must be on silent
- No audio/video recording without permission
- Keep discussion brief and to the point
- One representative per family for group issues

WHAT HAPPENS AT THE MEETING:
1. Security check at entrance
2. Register at reception with appointment ID
3. Wait in designated area
4. Personal Secretary calls your turn
5. Meet Collector, present your case
6. Collector gives instructions/orders
7. Receive written acknowledgment if needed

CANCELLATION POLICY:
- Cancel at least 24 hours before appointment
- Automatic cancellation if you don't show up
- Maximum 2 reschedules allowed
- No-show may result in temporary ban from portal

AVAILABLE TIME SLOTS:
- Tuesday to Friday: 11 AM - 1 PM, 3 PM - 5 PM
- Monday: Reserved for Public Hearing (walk-in)
- Weekends: Emergency only
- Government holidays: No appointments

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ABOUT APPOINTMENTS:
Q: How long will I wait for an appointment?
A: Usually 2-5 working days. Urgent cases may be accommodated sooner.

Q: Can I send a representative?
A: Yes, with a written authorization letter.

Q: Can I meet virtually?
A: Video conferencing available for differently-abled citizens and senior citizens.

Q: What if my issue isn't resolved after meeting?
A: Submit a follow-up complaint with the meeting reference number.
