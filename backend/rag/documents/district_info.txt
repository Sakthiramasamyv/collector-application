Coimbatore District - Official Government Information
=====================================================

District Overview:
Coimbatore is a major city and district in the western part of Tamil Nadu, India.
It is known as the "Manchester of South India" due to its textile industry.
The district is administered by the District Collector who is the head of the district administration.

District Collector's Office:
- Location: Collectorate, Coimbatore - 641 018
- Working Hours: Monday to Friday, 10:00 AM to 5:45 PM
- Public Grievance Day: Every Monday (10 AM - 1 PM)
- Phone: 0422-2390111
- Email: collector.cbe@nic.in

Key Departments Under District Collector:
1. Revenue Department - Land records, property tax, Patta transfers
2. Public Distribution System (PDS) - Ration cards, fair price shops
3. Social Welfare Department - Schemes for women, children, disabled
4. Rural Development - MGNREGS, Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana
5. Agriculture Department - Farmer support, crop insurance
6. Education Department - Schools, scholarships
7. Health Department - Government hospitals, health schemes
8. Disaster Management - Flood relief, drought assistance

How to File a Complaint:
Citizens can file complaints through:
1. This online portal (District Collectorate Assistance System)
2. In-person at the collectorate on public grievance day
3. CM Cell: 1100 (toll-free)
4. Tamil Nadu e-Sevai Centers

Types of Complaints Handled:
- Infrastructure (roads, water, electricity, drainage)
- Land and property disputes
- PDS and ration card issues
- Social welfare scheme issues
- Corruption complaints
- Environmental issues
- Public safety concerns
- Employment and labor issues

Response Time:
- Urgent complaints: 24-48 hours
- Regular complaints: 7-15 working days
- Complex cases: 30-60 days
