Government Welfare Schemes - Coimbatore District
==================================================

CENTRAL GOVERNMENT SCHEMES:

1. Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) - Housing for All
   - Beneficiaries: BPL families, EWS, LIG categories
   - Benefit: Financial assistance for house construction
   - How to Apply: Visit nearest CSC or collectorate
   - Documents needed: Aadhaar, income certificate, land documents

2. MGNREGS (Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Scheme)
   - Benefit: 100 days guaranteed employment per year per household
   - Wage: As per state minimum wage
   - How to Apply: Register at Gram Panchayat office
   - Contact: Block Development Officer (BDO)

3. PM Kisan Samman Nidhi
   - Beneficiaries: All land-holding farmer families
   - Benefit: Rs. 6000 per year in 3 installments
   - How to Apply: Through Agriculture Department or CSC

4. Ayushman Bharat - PM-JAY
   - Benefit: Health insurance of Rs. 5 lakh per family per year
   - Coverage: 1393 medical procedures covered
   - How to Apply: Hospitals empaneled under the scheme

STATE GOVERNMENT SCHEMES (Tamil Nadu):

1. Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thittam
   - Beneficiaries: Women heads of families
   - Benefit: Rs. 1000 per month financial assistance
   - How to Apply: Collectorate, Tahsildar office, or online portal

2. Chief Minister's Breakfast Scheme
   - Beneficiaries: Government school students (Classes 1-5)
   - Benefit: Free nutritious breakfast every school day

3. Varumun Kappom Thittam - Disease Prevention Scheme
   - Free health check-up camps in villages

4. Free Education Scheme
   - Free uniforms, textbooks, shoes, bags for government school students
   - Scholarships for SC/ST students

5. Amma Drinking Water Scheme
   - Safe drinking water for rural areas

6. NEEDS (New Entrepreneur-cum-Enterprise Development Scheme)
   - Beneficiaries: Youth (18-35 years), women
   - Benefit: Subsidized loans up to Rs. 25 lakh for new businesses

7. Tamil Nadu Housing Board Schemes
   - Affordable housing for EWS and MIG categories

8. Old Age Pension Scheme
   - Rs. 1000 per month for eligible senior citizens
   - Apply at Tahsildar office with age proof

9. Differently Abled Persons Welfare Schemes
   - Assistive devices, scholarships, reservations in employment
   - Disability certificates issued at District Medical Board

10. Widow Pension Scheme
    - Rs. 1000 per month for eligible widows
    - Apply at Tahsildar office

How to Check Scheme Eligibility:
- Visit Tamil Nadu Government portal: www.tn.gov.in
- Visit nearest e-Sevai center
- Contact District Social Welfare Office
- Call helpline: 1800-425-1234 (toll-free)

Document Requirements for Most Schemes:
- Aadhaar Card (mandatory)
- Income Certificate (from Tahsildar)
- Caste Certificate (for reserved category benefits)
- Residence Proof
- Bank Account details (Aadhaar-linked preferred)
