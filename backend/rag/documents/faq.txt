Frequently Asked Questions - District Collectorate Assistance
=============================================================

Q1: What is the role of the District Collector?
A: The District Collector is the chief administrative officer of the district. They oversee revenue administration, law and order, disaster management, development programs, and welfare schemes. They act as the link between the state government and the people.

Q2: How do I get an income certificate?
A: Visit your nearest Tahsildar office or e-Sevai center with Aadhaar card, ration card, and bank passbook. The certificate is issued within 7 working days. You can also apply online at tn.gov.in.

Q3: How do I apply for a caste certificate?
A: Apply at the Tahsildar office or e-Sevai center. Required documents: birth certificate, school certificate, parent's caste certificate, Aadhaar. Processing time: 15 working days.

Q4: How do I get a nativity certificate?
A: Visit Tahsildar office with Aadhaar, ration card, and proof of residence. Issued within 7 days.

Q5: What is the process for Patta transfer?
A: Apply at the Tahsildar office with original sale deed, previous Patta, encumbrance certificate, and ID proof. The process takes 30-90 days depending on complexity.

Q6: How do I get a new ration card?
A: Apply at the Tahsildar office or e-Sevai center. Documents: Aadhaar of all family members, family photo, proof of residence, income proof. Processing: 30 days.

Q7: What should I do if a government official asks for bribe?
A: Report immediately to the Vigilance and Anti-Corruption Department. You can also report through this portal. Your identity will be kept confidential. Evidence such as marked currency can be arranged with police assistance.

Q8: How do I report illegal construction?
A: File a complaint on this portal under 'Infrastructure/Land' category or contact the local municipality/panchayat. For DTCP violations, contact the District Town and Country Planning office.

Q9: What is CM Cell and how does it work?
A: CM Cell (1100) is a toll-free helpline to register complaints directly addressed to the Chief Minister's office. Complaints are forwarded to concerned departments with a mandatory response timeline.

Q10: How to report child labor?
A: Call Childline 1098 immediately. You can also report on this portal under 'Social Welfare'. The District Child Labor Inspector will take action within 24 hours.

Q11: Can I submit a complaint anonymously?
A: You can file without full details, but you must provide a valid mobile number for tracking. The collector's office maintains confidentiality of complainant information.

Q12: How do I check status of my complaint?
A: Use the 'Track Complaint' feature on this portal with your Ticket ID. You can also call the control room: 0422-2390111.

Q13: What if I'm not satisfied with the resolution?
A: You can escalate the complaint on this portal, contact CM Cell (1100), or file a complaint with the Tamil Nadu Information Commission for RTI-related matters.

Q14: Are there any schemes for unemployed youth?
A: Yes - NEEDS scheme for new entrepreneurs, Tamil Nadu Skill Development Corporation (TNSDC) training programs, and employment exchange registration. Visit the District Employment Office for details.

Q15: How to apply for disability certificate?
A: Apply at the District Medical Board (Government Hospital). Required: medical records, Aadhaar, passport photo. Assessment done by medical board. Certificate issued in 30 days.

Q16: What is the working hours of the Collectorate?
A: Monday to Friday: 10 AM to 5:45 PM. Saturday, Sunday, and Government holidays: Closed. Emergency services available 24x7 through control room.

Q17: How do I make a thanksgiving to a government official?
A: Use the 'Thanksgiving' section on this portal. Your appreciation will be officially recorded and forwarded to the concerned official and their department, which helps in their performance evaluation.

Q18: What documents are needed for flood/disaster relief?
A: Aadhaar card, ration card, photograph of damage, FIR (if applicable). Contact the District Disaster Management Authority office or report on this portal for immediate assessment.

Q19: How to get a death certificate?
A: Apply at the local Panchayat/Municipality with hospital death record or doctor's certificate. Issued within 7 days. Can also be obtained from e-Sevai centers.

Q20: What is the Collector's social media presence?
A: The District Collector's office publishes updates on the official district website and this portal. The collector may also deliver direct messages to citizens through the 'Collector's Messages' section of this portal.
