Complaint Filing and Resolution Guide
=======================================

TYPES OF COMPLAINTS ACCEPTED:

1. INFRASTRUCTURE COMPLAINTS
   - Road damage, potholes, broken footpaths
   - Street light failures
   - Drainage and sewage problems
   - Water supply issues (shortage, contamination)
   - Electricity supply problems (contact TANGEDCO separately for urgent)
   
2. LAND & REVENUE COMPLAINTS
   - Land encroachment
   - Patta transfer disputes
   - Survey number issues
   - Property tax disputes
   - Unauthorized construction

3. PUBLIC DISTRIBUTION SYSTEM (PDS) COMPLAINTS
   - Ration shop not opening on time
   - Short supply or adulteration of ration items
   - Ration card issues (new card, correction, deletion)
   - Denial of ration to eligible beneficiaries

4. CORRUPTION COMPLAINTS
   - Bribery by government officials
   - Misuse of public funds
   - Illegal land grabbing by influential persons
   These complaints are treated URGENT and investigated within 48 hours.

5. SOCIAL WELFARE COMPLAINTS
   - Delay in pension payments
   - Denial of welfare scheme benefits
   - Child labor, bonded labor complaints
   - Domestic violence (also contact: 181)

6. ENVIRONMENT COMPLAINTS
   - Illegal dumping of waste
   - Factory pollution (air, water, noise)
   - Deforestation complaints
   - River/lake encroachment

7. HEALTH COMPLAINTS
   - Government hospital negligence
   - Drug shortage in primary health centers
   - Fake medical practitioners
   - Food adulteration

HOW THE COMPLAINT PROCESS WORKS:

Step 1: Citizen files complaint with description, category, and contact info
Step 2: System assigns unique TICKET ID (format: CMP-XXXX)
Step 3: Complaint routed to concerned department automatically
Step 4: Department officer assigned within 24 hours (urgent) or 48 hours (normal)
Step 5: Field visit conducted if needed
Step 6: Resolution report submitted
Step 7: Citizen notified of resolution
Step 8: Citizen can rate the resolution (satisfied/not satisfied)

COMPLAINT PRIORITY LEVELS:
- CRITICAL: Life-threatening, corruption, major public safety (Response: 24 hours)
- HIGH: Infrastructure failure affecting large population (Response: 72 hours)  
- MEDIUM: Service delivery issues (Response: 7 days)
- LOW: Minor issues, suggestions (Response: 15 days)

1. Escalate within this portal
2. Contact CM Cell: 1100
3. Contact Tamil Nadu Information Commission
4. Approach High Court if needed
